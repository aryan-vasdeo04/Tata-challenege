3. How to use this in the case

A simple story to narrate while demonstrating the prototype.
  1. Start with a campaign reach assumption.
  2. Adjust engagement and qualification rates to show funnel leakage.
  3. Compare digital-only with digital + MFD assumptions.
  4. Use actual pilot results to decide which channel deserves more budget.

4. Guardrails before scaling

The calculator supports decisions; it does not replace evidence.
Unique investorsDeduplicate and verify completed first investments.
Incremental CACMeasure incremental investors, not just clicks or attributed leads.
Quality & complianceTrack retention, complaints, consent, disclosures and mis-selling signals.

Important: All conversion rates and CAC values in this prototype are hypothetical examples, not Tata AMC actuals or validated forecasts. The simplified spend estimate equals estimated investors × assumed CAC. A real business case should use gross acquisitions, channel-specific fully loaded costs, duplicates, attribution, attrition and retention, and should be approved through a controlled pilot.
